We believe in the next generation

Movement Kids is a safe, fun, faith-filled place for your kids! Every Sunday, they’ll experience worship, interactive lessons, and activities that bring the Bible to life. We believe church should be the best hour of their week as they grow in faith and friendships at Movement Kids!

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Any other questions? Email kids@wearemovement.ca

What should I expect when I arrive?

Movement Kids is all about creating a fun, exciting, and welcoming place where kids can know Jesus, grow in their faith, and build friendships! When you arrive, head to the Movement Kids Check-In Desk in the lobby. Our friendly team will get your child checked in and give you a security sticker with a number. Keep this sticker with you—you’ll need it when it’s time to pick up your child after the service. Once they’re checked in, it’s time to head to their class and get ready for a morning filled with fun, games, worship, Bible stories, and friends!

Is there something for my child with special needs?

At Movement Kids, we want every child to feel welcomed, loved, included, and part of the family! Our team works hard to create a safe and supportive environment where kids with different needs can participate, learn about Jesus, make friends, and have fun! We understand that church can sometimes feel overwhelming. If your child becomes overstimulated or needs some extra support, we have a sensory space in the corner of our Kids Church room. This gives children a quieter place to reset while still being able to see and take part in Kids Church. A Movement Kids leader will be there to supervise and support them. At Movement Kids, every child has a place to belong!

When does Movement Kids happen?

Movement Kids happens every Sunday at 9:00am and 11:00am!

How can I contact you?

You can contact kids@wearemovement.ca if you have any questions.

What do the kids do in their classes?

They experience age-appropriate Bible teaching, worship, fun activities, and learn about Jesus!
