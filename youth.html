What to expect

3 types of nights

01 Discipleship

Mentorship

Mentorship Nights are focused on helping students grow personally and spiritually through meaningful conversations. Instead of just hanging out, students connect intentionally with leaders and mentors who encourage, challenge, and guide them in their faith. These nights create space for honest questions, deeper reflection, and real discipleship so students feel known, supported, and strengthened in their walk with Jesus.

02 Opportunity

Worship

Worship Nights are dedicated to creating space to encounter God together through worship, prayer, and reflection. Students are invited to actively participate — not just attend — by leading, serving, sharing, and using their gifts. These evenings help students experience God’s presence in a deeper way while growing in confidence and leadership as worshippers.

03 Invite focused

Games

Games Nights are high-energy, fun, and welcoming events designed to help students build friendships and feel like they belong. Through creative themes, team challenges, and lots of laughter, these nights create an easy and comfortable environment for new students to connect. They’re perfect for inviting friends and building relationships that often lead to deeper conversations and spiritual growth.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

No questions match that. Email isaiah@wearemovement.ca and we’ll help.

What is Movement Youth?

Movement Youth is our weekly youth program for students in Grades 7–12. It’s a place to build real friendships, have fun, ask honest questions, and grow in faith together. Everyone is welcome — whether you’ve grown up in church or have never been before.

When does Movement Youth meet?

We meet every Wednesday from 6:30pm–8:30pm at Movement Church. Doors open at 6:30pm for check-in and hangout time.

Where is Movement Youth located?

Movement Church — 65 Lansdowne Ave, Woodstock, ON N4T 1S8

Is Movement Youth only for Christians?

Not at all. Movement Youth is for everyone. You don’t have to believe anything to come. You can show up, hang out, make friends, and explore faith at your own pace.

What happens on a typical night?

Every week is a little different, but most nights include:

  • Games and activities
  • Hanging out with friends
  • A short message about real life and faith
  • Small group discussions / worship / more games depending on the night
  • Time to connect and have fun

We keep things engaging, welcoming, and meaningful.

Is there a cost to attend?

Regular weekly youth nights are completely free. Some special events, retreats, or trips may have a cost, but we always communicate that clearly ahead of time.

What should I bring?

Just bring yourself — and maybe a friend 🙂 You don’t need to bring anything special. Dress comfortably and come ready to have fun.

What if it’s my first time?

We love first-time visitors. You can:

  • Fill out a Plan a Visit form ahead of time, or
  • Just show up and we’ll help you get checked in

Our team will greet you, help you feel comfortable, and introduce you to others.

How does check-in work?

For safety, all students check in when they arrive. New students will be registered when they come in, and returning students check in quickly each week. Parents or guardians can also provide emergency contact info during registration.

Is Movement Youth safe?

Yes. Student safety is a top priority.

  • All leaders are trained and screened
  • We follow clear supervision policies
  • Students are checked in and monitored throughout the night
  • Leaders are present in all areas
Can parents stay or observe?

Absolutely. Parents are always welcome to visit, meet leaders, or see what a night looks like. We love connecting with families.

How do I stay updated on events and announcements?

When you attend or fill out a Plan a Visit, you can sign up to receive:

  • Email updates
  • Text message reminders

This helps you stay informed about special events, schedule changes, and important announcements. You can unsubscribe anytime.

Who leads Movement Youth?

Movement Youth is led by Pastor Isaiah & Eve Belleth alongside trained adult leader volunteers who care deeply about students and their growth. Our team is passionate about creating a safe, fun, and welcoming environment for everyone.

How can I contact someone if I have questions?

We’d love to help.

  • Email: isaiah@wearemovement.ca
  • Phone: 519-537-2211

Or fill out the Plan a Visit form and we’ll reach out.

Can I bring a friend?

Yes — please do. Movement Youth is always better with friends. Everyone is welcome.
