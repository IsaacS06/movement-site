Find a Group Types of groups ↓
01

Interest Based Groups

Do what you love — with others who love it too.

Interest-Based Groups are built around shared passions like hiking, disc golf, board games, fitness, coffee, or creative arts. These groups are a fun, low-pressure way to build real friendships and find meaningful community through the things you already enjoy.

02

Bible Study Groups

Dig into God’s word, together.

Bible Study Groups are for anyone wanting to grow deeper in their understanding of Scripture beyond Sunday & Wednesday nights. Whether you’re new to the Bible or have studied it for years, these groups create space for honest questions, thoughtful discussion, and spiritual growth.

03

Small Groups

Where big church becomes small.

Small Groups are the heartbeat of Movement Church — a place to connect, pray, and walk through life with others. These groups meet regularly in homes or at the church and are focused on building deep relationships, sharing life, and growing in faith together.
