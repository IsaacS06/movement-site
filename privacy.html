Privacy Policy

How Movement Church collects, uses and protects your personal information when you use this website.

Last updated: October 20, 2026

On this page Who we are What we collect How we use it Services we use Cookies & analytics Children Keeping it safe Your choices Other websites Accessibility Contact us

Who we are

This website is run by Movement Church, 65 Lansdowne Ave, Woodstock, Ontario N4T 1S8. We take your privacy seriously and handle personal information in line with Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and other privacy laws that apply to us.

What we collect

Information you give us. When you fill out a form (for example Plan a Visit, Request Prayer, Talk with a Supporter, Join a Team, baptism sign-up, or a youth or kids registration), email us, or give online, we collect what you choose to share: usually your name, email, phone number, and any details you write in.

Information collected automatically. If you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics records general information such as the pages you visit, how long you stay, your browser and device type, and your approximate location (city level). It does not tell us who you are.

Things we don’t collect. We don’t sell, rent or trade your information, and we don’t use advertising cookies on this website.

How we use it

  • To respond to your questions, prayer requests and messages.
  • To help you plan a visit and get connected to ministries, groups and teams.
  • To keep children and students safe at check-in and pick-up.
  • To process donations and issue tax receipts.
  • To send updates you’ve signed up for (you can unsubscribe at any time).
  • To understand which parts of the website are helpful, and improve it.

We only share your information with church staff and trained volunteers who need it to help you, or when the law requires it.

Services we use

Some parts of this website are provided by other companies. When you use them, their own privacy policies also apply, and your information may be stored outside Canada.

  • Planning Center (Church Center) — our forms, events, groups and registrations.
  • Tithe.ly — online giving. We never see or store your full card or bank details.
  • Google — Google Analytics (only with your consent), Google Maps on our Visit page, and Google Fonts, which loads the website’s typefaces.
  • YouTube and Facebook — when you follow a link to watch online.

Cookies & analytics

Cookies are small files saved by your browser. This website only uses Google Analytics cookies, and only after you click Accept on our cookie banner. If you click Decline, Google Analytics doesn’t load at all.

We also save your cookie choice, and a note so our opening animation only plays once per visit, on your own device. These aren’t sent to us.

You can change your mind at any time: . You can also clear cookies in your browser’s settings.

Children

This website isn’t designed to collect information directly from children. Information about children (for example for Movement Kids check-in, Club 56 or Movement Youth registration) is provided by a parent or guardian and used only for their care and safety.

Keeping it safe

We use reasonable physical, technical and administrative safeguards to protect your information, and we keep it only as long as we need it for the purposes above or as required by law (for example, donation records for tax purposes).

Your choices

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it where we’re able to. You can also withdraw your consent to emails, texts or analytics at any time. Contact us using the details below and we’ll respond within 30 days.

If you have a concern we haven’t resolved, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Other websites

Our website links to other websites, including crisis lines, counselling services and book or parenting resources. We aren’t responsible for their content or privacy practices, and a link isn’t an endorsement of everything on them. In an emergency, call 911.

Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to use this website. It’s built to work with keyboards and screen readers, respects your device’s “reduce motion” setting, and aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 level AA. If something isn’t working for you, please tell us and we’ll fix it or get you the information another way.
